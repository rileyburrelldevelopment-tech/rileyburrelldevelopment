Support

Get help with EchoPath.

Report a bug, ask an accessibility question or send beta feedback to Riley Burrell Development.

Email supportRead the FAQ

Report a problem

Include your device model, operating-system version, EchoPath build/version and a short description of what happened. If possible, include the steps that caused the issue.

Accessibility feedback

If a control is difficult to find, VoiceOver wording is unclear, focus moves unexpectedly or information is not being announced, please report it as an accessibility issue.

Navigation & Scene Detection

When reporting guidance or visual-analysis problems, describe what you expected and what EchoPath reported. Do not put yourself at risk to reproduce a bug.

Beta feedback

When testing a TestFlight or development build, include the build number and explain whether the issue happens every time or only in a particular workflow.

Beta testers

TestFlight feedback is welcome.

You can use Apple’s TestFlight feedback tools or email support. Real-device reports are particularly useful because location, navigation, speech and on-device AI behaviour can differ from simulator testing.

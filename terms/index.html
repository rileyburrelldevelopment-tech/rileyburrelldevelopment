EchoPath Terms of Use

Last updated: 16 November 2026

By creating an account or using EchoPath, you agree to these Terms of Use. Please also read our Privacy Policy, which explains how personal information is handled.

1. Purpose of EchoPath

EchoPath is an assistive navigation and environmental-information tool. It is intended to provide additional information that may help users understand routes, locations and captured scenes.

2. Safety and navigation

EchoPath must not be treated as a replacement for personal judgement, orientation and mobility skills, a cane, guide dog, official travel information, emergency services or awareness of real-world surroundings.

GPS, route data, maps, place information and spoken guidance may be incomplete, delayed, unavailable or inaccurate. Always follow real-world conditions, signs, restrictions and safety instructions.

3. Scene Detection and AI

Scene Detection, object detection, OCR, colour analysis and AI-generated descriptions can be wrong. EchoPath may miss or misidentify objects, people, text, materials, colours, positions, obstacles or hazards. Do not rely on visual-analysis output as guaranteed safety information.

4. Beta software

EchoPath for iOS is offered through TestFlight and Android through an approved Google Play closed test. Beta access may be limited or withdrawn. Features may change, malfunction or be unavailable. Applicants must provide their Google Play account email and a brief reason for applying; approval is not guaranteed.

5. Accounts and sign-in

Creating an account involves providing a name and email address and using a supported sign-in method. A secure account service handles sign-in credentials, sessions and account identifiers. Optional verification methods may use a phone number or authenticator codes. You are responsible for protecting your sign-in details and for activity under your account.

6. Storage and cloud synchronisation

EchoPath may store saved places, addresses, photos, settings and pending changes on your device. For eligible signed-in users, supported cloud synchronisation is enabled by default: Supported saved records, settings, synchronisation information and saved-place photos are stored using cloud storage services. Turning off sync does not erase copies already stored online. You can manage available sync settings in the app.

7. Location, photos and online processing

Using navigation and nearby-place features permits EchoPath to process device location and send route requests, destinations and searches to mapping or routing services as needed. Using online photo analysis sends selected images and authentication information to EchoPath’s cloud service; photo questions may also include conversation context, and navigation photo checks may include location or destination context. Some analysis runs on-device. Camera, location and microphone permissions remain controlled by your device settings.

8. Account deletion and data

Deleting your account removes your sign-in identity. Removal of all related saved cloud records, photos, device copies, backups and separate support or beta emails is not currently confirmed as automatic. For help with associated personal information, contact rileyburrelldevelopment@gmail.com. Signing out is not the same as deleting stored information.

9. Purchases and communications

Optional supporter purchases, where available, are processed by Apple or Google under the applicable store terms. Store subscriptions must be managed through the relevant store. If you contact support or apply to test Android, we use the details you submit to respond and administer the request. See the Privacy Policy for more information.

10. Third-party services

Some EchoPath features depend on services supplied by Apple, Google or other platform providers. Availability and accuracy of those services are outside Riley Burrell Development’s direct control and may be subject to separate terms.

11. Availability

Features can vary by device, operating-system version, region, permissions, network connection and hardware capability. Photo analysis can take place on the device or through EchoPath’s cloud service. See the Privacy Policy for details.

12. Changes

EchoPath and these terms may change as the product develops. The latest terms will be published on this website.

13. Contact

Questions can be sent to rileyburrelldevelopment@gmail.com.

Features

Useful information before, during and around a journey.

EchoPath’s features are organised around practical tasks rather than technical complexity.

Features vary by platform and app version. Apply for the Android closed beta.

Location context

Where Am I

Read your current location and nearby named places. EchoPath can combine address information with nearby points of interest and speak a concise result when requested.

Designed for quick context

Manual Read actions are designed to speak when requested, even when automatic speech is reduced.

Places

Saved Places

Save locations that matter and start a new journey to them without searching again. Saved destinations can feed into the same travel-method and provider choices as address search.

Simple return journeys

Keep frequently used destinations easier to reach.

Safety

Safety tools

EchoPath includes safety-focused features such as Emergency Share and Safety Check-In, designed to provide extra support without claiming to replace emergency services or personal judgement.

Clear limitations

Safety information is presented cautiously and remains secondary to real-world awareness.

Search

Address and place search

On iOS, Place search can prioritise nearby results when location is available while still allowing searches for distant destinations.

Nearby first, not nearby only

Location is used as a preference rather than a hard boundary.

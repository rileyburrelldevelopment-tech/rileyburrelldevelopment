EchoPath Privacy Policy

How EchoPath handles your information.

Who we are

EchoPath is provided by Riley Burrell Development. For privacy questions or requests, email rileyburrelldevelopment@gmail.com.

Accounts and security

Email registration asks for your first and last names, email address and password. A secure account service processes sign-in credentials, manages sessions and assigns an account identifier used for synchronisation. Supported versions offer Sign in with Apple, email verification, password recovery and multi-factor authentication. If you choose SMS verification, your phone number is used to send and verify a code. Supported versions also offer authenticator app codes.

Location and navigation

With permission, EchoPath uses location for routing, guidance, nearby places and safety tools. Active guidance may use location in the background. Route and place lookups may send coordinates, destinations, searches and journey options to EchoPath’s routing service and mapping providers, including external mapping services. If you share your location, the app and recipients you choose receive it.

Where available, mapped crossing lookups send the search area to an external map-data service. EchoPath caches crossing results on the device.

Saved information and cloud synchronisation

EchoPath stores saved places, addresses, photos and preferences on your device. In versions with cloud sync, it is on by default for eligible signed-in accounts. Cloud storage services hold supported saved records, settings, synchronisation information and saved-place photos. Pending changes can remain on the device. You can manage synchronisation in Settings. Turning it off does not erase existing cloud copies. Supported paired watches can receive saved information and guidance data.

Photos, AI and voice input

Some visual analysis runs on the device. Online analysis sends the selected or captured image to EchoPath’s cloud service with an authentication token. Photo questions can include recent conversation context. Navigation photo checks can include destination or location details. The scene preview does not automatically save to Photos. Photos saved with places can be synchronised separately.

Voice input uses the platform’s speech recognition service. Recognised text follows the storage or transmission rules of the feature where you use it. Supported accessibility settings may sync.

Support and diagnostics

We use your contact details and message to respond to support requests. If you send diagnostics or attachments, they may be used to investigate the issue. Supported iOS builds can send these through the in-app support service. Android can open an email draft. Platform services such as TestFlight can provide beta and crash information.

Android closed beta applications

To apply, email us your Google Play account email address and a brief reason for joining. We use your message and sender details to review applications, contact you and administer the closed test. Feedback and support emails are used to respond and investigate issues. For questions about your application or correspondence, contact rileyburrelldevelopment@gmail.com.

Providers and purchases

EchoPath uses third-party cloud service providers for account management, synchronisation, data storage, app integrity, online photo analysis and routing. Apple, Google and other feature providers process information for maps, speech, app distribution and purchases. Where supporter purchases are offered, Apple or Google processes billing and EchoPath uses entitlement information to show or restore supporter status.

Retention and account deletion

Signing out ends your session but does not delete stored information. Deleting your account removes your sign-in identity. Automatic deletion of all related cloud records, saved photos, local copies, backups and separate beta or support emails is not currently confirmed. Contact rileyburrelldevelopment@gmail.com for help with associated information. Store subscriptions are managed separately through Apple or Google.

Your choices and rights

You can manage permissions through device settings. Some features need those permissions to work. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal data, restrict or object to its use, receive a copy, or withdraw consent where it applies. UK users can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
